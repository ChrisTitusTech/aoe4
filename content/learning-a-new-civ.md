Switching to a new civilization in Age of Empires IV typically results in a temporary ELO drop while you learn new mechanics and strategies. This guide breaks down the learning process into four clear phases to help manage your expectations.
<!--more-->

## The Learning Curve

**Total Timeline:** 2-3 months | **Required Games:** 100+

### Phase 1: Initial Learning (Weeks 1-2)
**ELO Impact: -100 to -200 points**

**Before Ranked:**
- Watch 2-3 civ guide videos from top players
- Study unique units and bonuses at [aoe4world.com/explorer](https://aoe4world.com/explorer)
- Beat Hardest AI before entering ranked
- Adjust hotkeys for new civ

**First 10-15 Ranked Games:**
- Master ONE build order
- Expect mistakes—focus on learning, not winning
- Take notes after each game

### Phase 2: Building Familiarity (Weeks 3-4)
**ELO Impact: -50 to -150 points**

- Learn 2-3 matchup-specific openings
- Understand your power spikes
- Identify favorable maps
- Practice adapting builds based on scouting

**Common Struggles:** Forgetting unique mechanics under pressure, inefficient use of special units, reverting to old habits

### Phase 3: Competency (Month 2)
**ELO Impact: -25 to -75 points**

- Comfortable with 3-4 strategies
- Adapt builds dynamically
- Understand landmark timings
- Know your matchups

**Focus:** Optimize resource management, practice advanced micro, learn counter-timings, review replays

### Phase 4: Mastery (Month 3+)
**ELO Impact: Return to baseline, potentially +50 to +100**

- Multiple refined builds per matchup
- Master all power spikes
- Confident improvisation
- Matchup-specific compositions perfected

## Essential Tips

**Mental Game:**
- Accept the ELO drop—it's temporary
- Set learning goals, not rank goals
- Treat losses as learning opportunities
- Don't switch back when frustrated

**Practice Smart:**
- Use unranked for experiments
- Focus on one aspect per game
- Create a personal cheat sheet
- Record and review your games

**Avoid These Mistakes:**
- Learning multiple civs at once
- Blaming the civ for losses
- Focusing only on gimmicks
- Copying pros without adapting

**Optimize Setup:**
- Adjust control groups for new units
- Set camera locations for unique buildings
- Practice unique mechanics in isolation
- Learn your "panic button" responses

## Reality Check

| Timeframe | Skill Level |
|-----------|-------------|
| 2 weeks (20 Games) | Basic mechanical errors |
| 1 month (40 Games) | Comfortable but unoptimized |
| 2 months (80 Games) | Solid competency |
| 3+ months (120+ Games) | Approaching mastery |

## Why It's Worth It

- Become a better overall player
- Learn how to counter the civ
- Prevent burnout with fresh gameplay
- Adapt better to meta shifts
- Often exceed your previous peak ELO

**Bottom Line:** Expect a 2-3 month journey. Your ELO will dip, but it's an investment in your long-term skill. Embrace the process!
