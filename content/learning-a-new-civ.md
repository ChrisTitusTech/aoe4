This is an overview guide for players looking to learn a new civilization in Age of Empires IV. Switching to a new civ can be a daunting task, often resulting in a significant temporary drop in ELO as you adjust to new mechanics, units, and strategies. This guide breaks down the learning process into manageable phases, providing expectations and tips to help you navigate the transition effectively.
<!--more-->
## Phase 1: Initial Learning (Week 1-2)

**Expected ELO Impact: -100 to -200 points**

### Off-Ladder Preparation

- Watch 2-3 civilization guide videos from top players
- Read through all unique units, buildings, and bonuses 
  - Check out <https://aoe4world.com/explorer>
- Practice build orders in skirmish mode vs. AI
  - Typically I never enter ladder until I can defeat the AI on Hardest difficulty
- Learn hotkey adjustments specific to the civilization

### Early Ladder Games (First 10-15 matches)

- Focus on executing ONE standard build order consistently
- Expect mechanical mistakes and timing issues
- Don't worry about win rate—focus on learning
- Take notes after each game on what felt awkward

## Phase 2: Building Familiarity (Week 3-4)

**Expected ELO Impact: Stabilization, still -50 to -150 from baseline**

### Developing Fundamentals

- Learn 2-3 matchup-specific openings
- Understand your civilization's power spikes
- Identify which maps favor your new civ
- Start recognizing when to deviate from build orders

### Common Struggles

- Forgetting unique mechanics in the heat of battle
- Reverting to old civ habits under pressure
- Inefficient use of unique units/abilities
- Unclear army compositions for different matchups

## Phase 3: Competency (Month 2)

**Expected ELO Impact: -25 to -75 from baseline**

### Mid-Level Mastery

- Comfortable with 3-4 different strategies
- Can adapt builds based on scouting information
- Understanding unique landmark choices and timing windows
- Recognizing favorable and unfavorable matchups

### Refinement Focus

- Optimize resource management for civ-specific bonuses
- Practice advanced micro with unique units
- Learn counter-timings against popular strategies
- Review replays to identify inefficiencies

## Phase 4: Full Integration (Month 3+)

**Expected ELO Impact: Return to baseline, potentially +50 to +100**

### Advanced Play

- Multiple refined build orders per matchup
- Strong understanding of all power spikes
- Confident improvisation when strategies fail
- Matchup-specific unit compositions mastered

## Key Tips for Civ Switching

### Mental Preparation

- **Accept the ELO drop is temporary and normal**
- Set learning goals, not rank goals
- Treat losses as data collection, not failures
- Don't switch back to your main civ when frustrated

### Practice Strategies

- Use unranked/custom lobbies for experimental builds
- Focus on one aspect per game (e.g., "today I master sacred site timing")
- Create a personal cheat sheet of civ-specific reminders
- Record your games to review decision-making

### Avoiding Common Pitfalls

- Don't learn multiple new civs simultaneously
- Resist the urge to blame the civ for losses
- Don't over-focus on unique gimmicks; learn solid fundamentals first
- Avoid trying to copy pro players exactly—adapt to your skill level

### Optimization Tips

- Adjust control groups for new unit types
- Create civ-specific camera locations for unique buildings
- Practice unique mechanics in isolation (e.g., HRE prelate management)
- Learn the civ's "panic button" when under early pressure

## Timeline Reality Check

**Realistic Expectations:**

- **2 weeks:** Still making basic mechanical errors
- **1 month:** Comfortable but not optimized
- **2 months:** Solid competency in most situations
- **3+ months:** Mastery approaching your main civ level

## Motivation Reminders

- Learning a second civ makes you a better overall player
- You gain insight into how to beat that civ
- Fresh gameplay prevents burnout
- Long-term, versatility helps you adapt to meta shifts
- The ELO you lose will come back—and often you'll exceed your previous peak

**Bottom Line:** Plan for 2-3 months and 100+ games to reach true competency. Your ELO will dip significantly at first, but this is the price of expanding your skillset. Embrace the learning process!
