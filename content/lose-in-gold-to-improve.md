Losing games in Gold league can be frustrating, but it's often a necessary step toward improvement in Age of Empires IV. This guide outlines strategies to embrace losses and why you should not just find the meta build orders to climb quickly.
<!--more-->

## Embrace the Learning Process

Losing in Gold league is a sign that you're pushing your limits and learning new skills. Each loss provides valuable feedback on what areas need improvement, whether it's macro management, micro control, or strategic decision-making. Instead of focusing solely on winning, prioritize learning from each game.

## Make your own builds for each type of opponent

Creating your own build orders tailored to different opponents can significantly enhance your gameplay. Analyze common strategies used by players in Gold league and develop counter-builds that exploit their weaknesses. This personalized approach will not only improve your adaptability but also deepen your understanding of the game mechanics.

### Types of Opponents to Prepare For:
- **Aggressive Rushers:** Focus on early defense and counter-attacks.
- **Turtlers:** Develop strong economy-focused builds to outpace them.
- **Boomers:** Prepare for mid-game aggression with well-timed military production.

Prepare your own modifications to builds to counter these playstyles effectively.

### How to Prepare:
1. **Study Replays:** Watch your own games and those of higher-ranked players to identify common strategies.
2. **Experiment:** Test different build orders in unranked matches to see what works best against various playstyles.
3. **Adapt:** Be ready to adjust your strategy mid-game based on your opponent's actions.
4. **Understand Win Conditions:** Know what it takes to win against each type of opponent and tailor your builds accordingly.
5. **Timing is Key:** Practice the timing of your build orders to ensure you hit critical milestones before your opponent can capitalize on their strategy.

## Avoid Meta Build Orders

While meta build orders can help you climb the ranks quickly, they often lead to a shallow understanding of the game. Relying solely on these strategies can hinder your growth as a player. Instead, experiment with different civilizations and playstyles to develop a deeper comprehension of game mechanics and strategies.
